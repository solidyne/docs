## Monitor Section

This section contains the controls:

- **Studio Monitor**: determines which signal will be heard on the studio monitor speakers and headphones.
- **Control Monitor**: assigns the signal to the control room monitor speakers and headphones.
- **CUE volume** control.
- **Talk-Back** button and microphone to the Studio.
- Master "**ON-AIR Mics**" button. Activates the microphone channels and "ON-AIR" signals.

### **Studio Monitor**

- Provides four signal sources: CUE, PGM, REC and ON-AIR \[4\].
- The console sends signals with fixed level to both headphones and speakers.
- For level distribution and control in the Studio, a **headphone distribution amplifier** is required, such as the optional Solidyne HD3 and HD5 modules.

<Info>
  See wiring in Installation ⟶ [Monitoring](/en-US/DX816/816-monitoring)
</Info>

### **Monitor Control**

- Headphone level [3] and speaker level [1] are controlled independently.
- Use the selector buttons [5] to choose the signal to monitor:
  - **PGM**: signal sent on-air.
  - **REC**: signal from the REC auxiliary bus.
  - **ON-AIR**: signal from an external tuner, recommended for on-air monitoring.

<Note>
  Adjust monitor speaker volume to a comfortable level with the **SPEAKERS** control in center position.
</Note>

<Info>
  If the Studio-Transmitter Link or the audio processor introduces audible delay, it's recommended to process the **ON-AIR** monitor signal with a delay-free processor connected directly to a PGM output from the console.
</Info>

## CUE Monitoring

Each channel features a **CUE** button that allows monitoring the signal before the fader (off-air). When multiple CUE signals are activated, they are summed together.

The CUE signal can be routed to:

- **Control Room monitor speakers**: press the **MIX** button \[2\].
- **Control Room headphones**. This routing is fixed. CUE is summed with the signal selected by the monitoring source.
- Studio monitor. Available as a signal source in STUDIO MONITOR.

CUE level is controlled exclusively by the **CUE** knob \[6\], affecting both headphones and speakers.

<Warning>
  If the **CUE \[6\]** knob is turned down, the CUE signal will not be heard even if the headphone and speaker knobs are turned up.

  Conversely, if **SPEAKERS \[1\]** and **HEADPHONES \[3\]** are turned down, CUE will still be audible if the CUE knob is turned up.
</Warning>

Additional features:

- CUE routing is **mono**.
- To isolate CUE monitoring:
  - Reduce the monitoring level of **SPEAKERS** or **HEADPHONES** as appropriate.
  - Release PGM, REC and ON-AIR buttons.

<Tip>
  We recommend monitoring the on-air signal at moderate volume and CUE at a higher level to clearly distinguish between them.
</Tip>

## Studio Talkback

The talkback channel allows the operator to communicate with the announcer in the studio.

- Press the **TALK-BACK** button.
- Program audio in the control room speakers is muted to prevent feedback.
- The operator is heard on the studio's **left channel**.
- The **right channel** maintains the on-air signal as a reference for announcers and reporters.
- You can also activate **CUE** on the microphone channel to communicate with the announcer.

<Note>
  The talkback microphone level is factory-calibrated.\
  It should only be adjusted when absolutely necessary, using the **LEVEL** preset.
</Note>
