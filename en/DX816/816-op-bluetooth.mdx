## Using with Mobile Phones (Bluetooth)

To operate a phone on the console, it must be [paired with the Bluetooth device](/es/DX816/816-telco) TELCO.

### Quick Reconnection

1. Secure the **TELCO** fader to **CUE** (closed).
2. **Enable Bluetooth on the console:** hold **BLUETOOTH for approximately 5 seconds** and release as soon as **CELL PHONE** lights up blue. The button continues to flash with a slow blink.

<Warning>
  The Bluetooth module receives power from the **USB-1 or USB-2** channels.\
  If neither is connected to a PC, use an **external USB power source** (a standard charger) with the supplied cable.
</Warning>

3. Activate Bluetooth on the mobile phone → the pairing is restored.
4. Incoming calls can be answered from the mobile phone or with **BLUETOOTH** on the console.

### Incoming Call (Mobile Phone)

- The phone rings in **CUE** (with TELCO fader closed). During the ring, the CELL PHONE button stops blinking and remains off.
- Answer with **short press on BLUETOOTH** (or from the mobile phone screen).
- Press the Bluetooth button to take audio from the mobile phone. It will blink red/pink.
- **Preview conversation:** With fader in **CUE**, the call is heard through **CUE**.
- **Press TALK** to speak. The button is latched. While active, only the talkback microphone signal will be sent to the phone. Press again when finished speaking.
- **HOLD**: Move the fader to the HOLD zone, the caller hears the program but does not go on-air.
- **ON-AIR**: Raise the fader to reach the appropriate level.

<Info>
  In conferences between **mobile phone** and **land line (POTS)/VoIP**, there may be some **echo** in the return from the remote journalist. It **does not** go on-air.
</Info>

### Ending the Communication

- **Short press BLUETOOTH** on the console, or disconnect from the phone.

### Return to Mobile

- On the mobile phone: exit **Headset/Bluetooth** mode by switching to **Speaker**.
- To return to the console: switch back to **Headset** mode (varies by phone model).

### Make a call from mobile phone

1. On the mobile phone, disconnect Bluetooth to speak directly from the phone.
2. Establish the call.
3. To transfer to console, switch to **Bluetooth/Headset** mode.
4. With the fader in **HOLD** position, the mobile phone receives return audio from the console but does not go on-air.
5. Raise the fader to put on-air.
6. To end call: **brief press of BLUETOOTH button** or disconnect from the mobile phone.

<Note>
  The call can also be established **without leaving** Bluetooth mode on the mobile phone, with the fader in **CUE** position and using **TALK** to communicate from the console.
</Note>
