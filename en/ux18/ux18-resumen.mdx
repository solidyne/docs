This section describes features that _only apply to the UX18 model_. Also refer to the [Unidex Series](/en/unidex/unidex-overview) for more information.

![UX18 Pn](/images/thumbnails/UX18.png)

## Channels

UNIDEX UX18 has 18 channels:

- **10 channels** with 100 mm faders and **8 "virtual" channels** that are operated from the touch screen or via remote web interface.
- 4 channels can work as **groups**, to control **virtual channel submixes** from physical faders.
- Each channel can be assigned to any input.
- Channels are configured in three modes: **MIC**, **LINE**, or **TELCO**, which modifies their behavior and functions.
- They can be assigned to four stereo mix buses: **PGM**, **REC**, **AUX**, and **VBUS**.

## Inputs

The UX18 features 5 microphone inputs and 13 line inputs (analog and digital).

- **Microphone: 5 mono balanced**
  With 48V phantom power, 4-band parametric equalizer, dynamic compressor, noise gate, and de-esser.
- **Line: 2 stereo balanced**
  With 3-band parametric equalizer.
- **UNIDEX: 8 mono (4 stereo)**
  Balanced inputs configurable as mono or stereo.
  Allow connection of UDX adapters (microphones, phone lines, mobile phones via Bluetooth, computers via USB).
  Include 3-band parametric equalizer, dynamic compressor, and noise gates.
- **USB: 4 stereo playback devices**
  Compatible with ASIO 3.0.
  Include 3-band parametric equalizer.
- **AES3: 2 stereo digital inputs**
  With 3-band parametric equalizer.
- **Incoming streaming**
  Not an independent input; when activated, it replaces AES3 input #1.
- **External tuner**
  Stereo input for transmission monitoring. It can also be assigned to channels to be used as a conventional input.

## Outputs

There are 9 stereo outputs and monitoring outputs:

- **Program (PGM)**
  Dual balanced analog stereo output.
- **1 balanced stereo REC output**
- **4 unbalanced stereo outputs (assignable)**
  Can be configured as up to 8 mono outputs. Include a **mix-minus** option for telephone and VoIP communications.
- **USB: 4 stereo recording devices**
- **AES3: 2 digital outputs**
  Two of them are assignable to any of the buses.
- **Dual PCM/MP3/OPUS streaming**
  Simultaneous transmission via RTP and Icecast/Shoutcast.

## Monitoring and Intercom

- Monitoring outputs for the Studio (speakers and headphones).
- Simplified connection for Studio monitoring with **Solidyne StudioBox** bays.
- Monitoring outputs for the Control room (speakers and headphones).
- Talkback to the Studio.
- Selectable command microphone: internal (PZM) or external.
- Reverse talkback: allows speaking from the Studio to the operator's CUE (requires Studiobox HD3 or HD5).
- CUE bus (Pre Fader Level) assignable to any output.

## External Device Triggering

It features:

- **2 GPI**
- **7 GPO** of the open-collector type

They allow triggering:

- Solidyne Audicom automation
- Audio processors
- Other devices with remote control

Activation is programmable (see the corresponding section).
