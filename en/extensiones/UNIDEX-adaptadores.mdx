The **UDX external adapters** allow expanding the capabilities of UNIDEX consoles to handle various types of signals and devices.

They connect simply using RJ45-RJ45 cables. Each UDX adapter requires connection to a UNIDEX input. Some UDX models also use console outputs.

All UNIDEX adapter functions **are controlled from the console**.

Available modules are:

## UDX Boxes Installation

**RJ45** connections are highly reliable, but require certain conditions to ensure proper installation.

- It is essential that the boxes and cables are securely fastened, preventing any movement in the connector.
- Cables must be installed in **conduits** or **cable channels**, ensuring they are not loose or subjected to mechanical stress.
- The boxes should be firmly mounted to the wall or under tables.\
  They feature **two notches** that allow them to be secured with screws and easily removed if needed.

    Download [drilling template](https://solidynepro.com/manuales/udx-drilling-template.pdf) for anchor screws.
