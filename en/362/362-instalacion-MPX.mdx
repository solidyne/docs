The **Audimax 362HD** features dual MPX output with independent level adjustments.\
This allows connection to a second transmitter operating as a backup, or alternatively, to a transmitter used during nighttime.

- The output connectors are of the **BNC** type.
- For MPX connection, **75 Ohm** coaxial cable (CCTV standard) must be used.
- The cable length should not exceed **25 meters**.

<Warning>
  It is essential to maintain proper grounding distribution.
</Warning>

<Note>
  When connecting to the transmitter via the MPX input, ensure that the transmitter's internal pre-emphasis network is disabled (flat response 20 – 100 kHz).
  The pre-emphasis in the Audimax 362HD is factory-set, with the option to choose between 50 µs or 75 µs.
</Note>

## Modulation Adjustment to 100%

Modulation is adjusted by varying the MPX output level of the unit.\
It's recommended to use program material with **loud voice and music** (example: contemporary pop).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Prepare the input signal">
    - Ensure you're using the appropriate inputs (BAL/UNBAL) according to the console.
    - Adjust the audio level on the console to reach **0 VU on peaks**.
    - This way, the AGC stage operates at its equilibrium point.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the processor and transmitter">
    - Set all processor controls to **center position**.
    - If the transmitter has modulation level control, set it to maximum.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust MPX output">
    - Vary the 362HD's MPX output level until achieving **75 kHz deviation (100%)**, measured with an **FM modulation monitor** or the transmitter's indicator instrument.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Some countries permit occasional peaks up to **110% modulation** (example: FCC regulations in the USA).\
  Needle-type modulation indicators may show peaks above 100% due to ballistic overshoot or average sine wave readings. This doesn't indicate actual over-modulation in the system.
</Note>

Once the modulation level is adjusted, proceed to **customize the station's sound** by adjusting the Audimax 362HD controls.\
This process doesn't affect the modulation level, as the processor delivers constant peak level.
