The **Solidyne Audimax 362HD processor** integrates **7 processing stages** plus a **stereo coder** in a single chassis.\
Its main feature is **ease of use**, as it doesn't require a specialized technician for adjustments.

- It has no manual input level adjustment. It incorporates **high-level balanced inputs** (XLR and RJ45) and **low-level unbalanced inputs (RCA)**.
- Its **automatic gain control (AGC)** adapts the equipment's input by compensating level differences across a wide range (30 dB).

The ability to operate without pre-emphasis/de-emphasis also makes it an excellent processor for **WEBcasting** (streaming).

The processor operates in **3 bands**, fully controlled via **VCA (Voltage Controlled Amplifiers)**, simplifying its adjustment.

The **stereo coder** uses **digital synthesis with 16x oversampling**, a technology developed by Solidyne that ensures:

- Ultra-low distortion.
- High channel separation.
- Operation free from readjustments throughout its lifespan.

The **MPX output** to the transmitter is differential type, canceling any potential residual hum in the signal.
