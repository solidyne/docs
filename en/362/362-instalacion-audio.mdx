## Digital Input/Output via USB

The **Audimax 362HD/USB** model includes an optional module for sending and receiving audio via a standard USB A/B cable connection to a PC.

- When connected to a **<sup>Windows©</sup>** computer, the system automatically recognizes the processor and installs the necessary drivers.\
  No additional drivers are required.
- The unit is recognized as a **stereo audio card**:
  - **USB playback device** → corresponds to the **audio input to the processor**.
  - **USB capture device** → corresponds to the **audio output from the processor**.

### About USB Detection

- Before connecting the USB port, ensure that the **console and the PC are properly grounded** through three-prong power plugs.

<Danger>
  Verify with a tester (25 V AC scale) between the PC chassis and the console chassis.\
  The reading should be 0 V. Otherwise, the USB input of the processor or the computer could be damaged.
</Danger>

- Avoid changing USB port cables, as Windows may reassign the order of devices.

## HD Mode (High Definition)

The **HD mode** is designed for audio applications where the FM stereo encoding stage (MPX) is not used.

- Fully digital stations (HD Radio and streaming).
- Sound reinforcement applications (small venues, auditoriums, shopping centers).

**When switching to HD mode:**

- The FM emphasis network is deactivated (and therefore the de-emphasis of the audio outputs).
- A **fast-acting limiter** is enabled on the outputs.
- The **MPX** outputs are deactivated.

### Enable HD Mode

To change the operating mode, it is necessary to access the **internal micro-switches** by removing the top cover of the unit.

- All switches **ON** = FM Mode
- All switches **OFF** = HD Mode

The status is indicated on the front panel by the **“FM”** LED:

- Lit = FM
- Off = HD

<Info>
  The unit is factory-configured in **FM mode**.
</Info>
