## Key Concepts

In operation, it is crucial to distinguish three distinct actions:

-   **Enable/Disable Bluetooth on the mobile phone**\
    Turn Bluetooth on or off on the phone. This does not imply pairing or connecting to a device.
-   **Pair the mobile phone with a device**\
    Also called *pairing*. The mobile phone **registers** an external device (headphones, car hands-free kit, or the MX2200's Bluetooth channel). This is done **only once** per device.\
    A device can be **unpaired**, meaning it is deleted from the list of Bluetooth devices paired to the mobile phone. To use it again, it must be paired anew.
-   **Connect/Disconnect a previously paired device**\
    The mobile phone **connects** to one of the previously paired devices. This is different from pairing. With Bluetooth enabled on the mobile phone, you connect or disconnect from the **list of paired devices**.

### BLT LED Status

The **BLT** button lights up in amber with different flashing patterns depending on the Bluetooth status:

| Mode | Status                                    | LED Indication                           | Meaning                                                                                                                                                   |
| :--: | ----------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **A** | **Mobile phone connected**                | Short on (1 s) + long off (2 s)          | The mobile phone is connected to the MX2200, **with no call in progress**                                                                                 |
| **B** | **Mobile phone not connected / Call in progress** | Slow on (2 s) + short off (1 s) | - Bluetooth is on but **no mobile phone is connected**<br />- Or the mobile phone is on an active **call**<br />- When a phone connects, status changes to **A** |
| **C** | **_Pairing Mode_**                        | Fast flash                               | Allows the phone to find and **pair** with the **MX2200**                                                                                                 |
| **D** | **Incoming call**                         | Steady on                                | Indicates an incoming call                                                                                                                                |
| —    | **Off**                                   | No illumination                          | Bluetooth is off                                                                                                                                          |

### Operation Notes

- **Mode A** confirms that the mobile phone is paired correctly, ready to be used as an audio source or backup link.
- **Mode B** can indicate two situations: that no mobile phone is connected yet, or that there is an active call.
- **Mode C** should only be used when **pairing** a new phone.
- **Mode D** acts as a clear incoming call notification, with a steady illumination.

<Note>
  Never confuse **pairing (mode C)** with **connecting/disconnecting (mode A/B)**. Pairing is only performed once per phone, while connection/disconnection occurs each time it is used.
</Note>

## Pairing a Mobile Phone with the MX2200

<Steps>
  <Step title="Prepare the Console">
    Ensure Bluetooth is **off** on the MX2200.\
    If the **BLT** button flashes **slowly** in amber (≈1 flash every 2 s), Bluetooth is **on**. Hold **BLT** until the LED turns **off** completely.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Pairing Mode">
    With Bluetooth off, hold **BLT** for ~**8 s**.\
    You will first see **slow** flashes (mode B) and then a **fast** flash (mode C). **Release** the button when it changes to fast flash: the console is now in **pairing** mode and ready to be discovered.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search from the Mobile Phone">
    On the phone, **enable Bluetooth** and search for **new devices** (the procedure varies by brand/model).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the Device">
    The device will appear as **Solidyne BTM641**. Select it to **pair** and then to **connect** if the system prompts twice.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirmation and Naming">
    After pairing, **BLT** will have a **slow** flash (Bluetooth **active**).\
    On the mobile phone, you can **rename** "Solidyne BTM641" from the device settings, if desired.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Multiple Phones and Distance">
    You can **pair several** phones with the MX2200, but **only one** can be **connected** at a time.\
    Typical range: **up to 3 m**. Keep the phone **close**, but **never** resting directly on the MX2200.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Reconnecting a previously paired mobile phone

The mobile phone may **disconnect** from the MX2200 in various situations. The following table indicates what happens and how to reconnect it:

| Situation                                            | What happens to the call                                                                             | How to return to MX2200                                                                                             |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Bluetooth is turned off on the mobile phone**                   | Typically the call **is transferred to the phone** (behavior may vary by brand/model) | Turn **Bluetooth back on**; reconnection may be **automatic** or you may need to select **Solidyne BTM641** from the list |
| **The mobile phone is disconnected from the Bluetooth device** | The call leaves Bluetooth and goes to the **phone**                                                 | On the mobile phone, **reconnect to the Bluetooth** device Solidyne BTM641 to return it to the console.                            |
| **"Speaker" is activated on the mobile phone**                  | The call leaves "hands-free" mode (Bluetooth) and goes to the **phone**                          | Switch to **"Headset/Bluetooth"** on the mobile phone to return to the console                                            |
| **The Bluetooth channel is turned off on the MX2200**            | The Bluetooth link is cut. Typically the call goes to the phone.                              | **Turn BLT on** and **reconnect** from the mobile phone                                                                  |

<Note>
  Reconnection **will not be possible** if the **same** Bluetooth channel on the MX2200 was paired with **another phone** and that second phone is currently **connected**.
</Note>

## Volume Adjustment

<Warning>
  On the **mobile phone**, set the **Bluetooth volume to maximum** to ensure good reception and the best **signal-to-noise ratio**.
</Warning>

<Info>
  Make the adjustment **during a Bluetooth call**. If you change the volume **without** an active call, you are adjusting the phone's internal **speaker volume** and **not** the Bluetooth volume.
</Info>

- The Bluetooth level is **stored on the mobile phone**.
- If you change phones, repeat the adjustment to set the Bluetooth volume **to maximum**.

## Turn Off Bluetooth on the Console

To **turn off** Bluetooth on the MX2200, hold down **BLT** until the LED **turns off**.\
If operating on battery and you will not use Bluetooth, keep it **off** to **save power**.

## On-air Operation via Bluetooth

<Steps>
  <Step title="Console Preparation">
    In **CELL PHONE**, keep the **LEVEL control closed** and **CUE/PGM in CUE** (released).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable Bluetooth on the Console">
    Press and hold **BLT** for ~**5 s** until it remains **illuminated** with a **slow flash** (Bluetooth active).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect the Mobile Phone">
    Enable Bluetooth on the **mobile phone**. If it was already paired, the link is restored in **a few seconds**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Before **transferring** the call to the console, verify that **CUE/PGM is in CUE** to prevent the call from going **on-air** at the moment of transfer.
</Warning>

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Incoming Call (Bluetooth)">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Answer if Bluetooth is Off">
        If Bluetooth is **off on the mobile phone**, answer the call from the **phone** and then **enable Bluetooth** to transfer it to the console.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Answer if Bluetooth is Active">
        If Bluetooth is **active**, you can answer directly from the console with a **short tap on BLT**.\
        In this condition, the conversation takes place **only through the console**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Call Transfer">
        Once transferred to the console, the **mobile phone receives the PGM signal**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Private Talk">
        Press **TALK** in the **CELL Phone** zone and listen via **CUE** on the headphones.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Put to PGM">
        Change **CUE/PGM to PGM** and **adjust the level** to send the call to program.
      </Step>
      <Step title="End the Call">
        End with a **short tap on BLT** or by hanging up from the **mobile phone**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Resume from the Phone">
        To return the call to the mobile phone, change the mode from **"Headset/Bluetooth"** to **"Speaker"** on the phone.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Call from the Mobile Phone">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Switch to Speaker">
        On the **mobile phone**, set the audio to **"Speaker"** to speak from the phone.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Dial the Call">
        Perform the **dialing** and establish the call from the mobile phone.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Transfer to the Console">
        Change the audio mode on the mobile phone to **"Headset/Bluetooth"** to pass the call to the console.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Confirm Not in PGM">
        Before transferring, ensure the console is in **CUE**.\
        If it were in **PGM**, the call could accidentally go on-air.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Put On-air">
        Change **CUE/PGM to PGM** and **adjust the level** to send the call to program.
      </Step>
      <Step title="End the Call">
        End with a **short tap on BLT** or by hanging up from the **mobile phone**.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
