Step-by-step procedure for receiving or placing a call on the console

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="RECEIVE A CALL">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Identify the incoming call">
        When receiving a call, the **HOLD** button flashes at the ring rate, regardless of the position of the **PHONE/LINE IN** button.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Verify channel in CUE">
        Ensure **PGM/CUE** is in the **CUE** position (released) before answering the line.

        <Warning>
          If **PGM/CUE** is in **PGM** when answering, the call is added **directly to the output mix**, with the level set by the **LEVEL** knob.
        </Warning>
      </Step>
      <Step title="Select input">
        The **PHONE/LINE IN** button must be in the **PHONE** position (released) to listen to the phone line audio and for the line to receive the mix-minus return.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Answer the call">
        Press **HOLD**. The caller will hear the **PGM** signal as the mix-minus return.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Adjust headphone level">
        Adjust with the line's **LEVEL** knob.\
        The overall **CUE MONITOR** level adjustment also affects this.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Speak privately">
        Press the corresponding microphone **TALK** button.\
        While held down, the caller stops hearing the program mix-minus and hears only the operator.\
        When released, the line returns to hearing the program signal.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Monitor without program">
        In the **CUE MONITOR** section, the **PGM** button allows you to remove program audio from the headphones and listen only to the phone line.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Put on-air">
        Press **PGM/CUE**.\
        The level in the mix is adjusted with the **LEVEL** knob.\
        The caller will be able to converse with the four active microphones on the console.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Hold the line">
        To take the call off-air and put it on hold:

        - Turn the **LEVEL** knob to zero.
        - Press **CUE/PGM** to return to CUE.\
          The line is held, listening to the program signal.
      </Step>
      <Step title="End the call">
        Press **HOLD** to hang up and release the line.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="PLACE A CALL">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Verify channel in CUE">
        Confirm that the **PGM/CUE** button is in **CUE** (released).
      </Step>
      <Step title="Take the line">
        Press **HOLD**.\
        The dial tone will be heard in the headphones.\
        The volume is adjusted with the **LEVEL** knob and the overall **CUE MONITOR** control.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Dial">
        Use the **console keypad**.\
        If you need to hang up and redial: release **HOLD** and press it again to retake the line.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Continue operation">
        Once the call is established, continue from the step **Speak privately** (see the *Receive a Call* section, step 6).
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
