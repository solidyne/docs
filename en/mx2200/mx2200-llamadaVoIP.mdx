The **SIP CALL** option enables establishing a **bidirectional communication or link** over the Internet with **minimal delay**.\
It is equivalent to a conventional VoIP call (similar to Skype or WhatsApp).

It is used when required:

- Immediate interaction with the studios.
- Connection simplicity on networks over which you have no control (public Internet, Wi-Fi from various locations).

The communication is established without complex configurations at the endpoints, even under **limited bandwidth** conditions.

- Configured at a low bitrate, it ensures continuity even with restricted Internet.
- Configured at maximum quality, the audio is equivalent to direct sound in the studios.

Usage is simple: a pre-loaded address book shows the available contacts. The user selects one and makes the call. In the studio, the operator receives the communication on a computer or compatible equipment. It is also possible for the MX2200 to **receive the incoming call originated from the Studio**.

## MX2200 Reception at the Studios

The SIP call can be received with any compatible system:

- **Hardware DECODERs**.
- **VoIP** software on a PC.

The user can freely choose the SIP service:

- Paid services are recommended for stability.
- Free options also exist, with [Linphone](https://www.linphone.org) being a notable one.

The **SIP account/contact** assigned to the studio (the contact the console calls) is configured on the decoder.
