This section describes functionalities that _only apply to the UX24 model_. Also refer to the [Unidex Series](/en/unidex/unidex-summary) for more information.

![UX24 Pn](/images/thumbnails/UX24.png)

## Channels

- The UNIDEX UX24 has 24 channels:
  12 channels with 100 mm faders and 12 "virtual" channels that are operated from the touch screen or via remote web interface.
- It can be expanded with UX6 modules to provide 18 or 24 physical faders.
- Each channel can be assigned to any input (except the external tuner).
- Channels are configured in three modes: **MIC**, **LINE**, or **TELCO**, which modifies their behavior and functions.
- They can be assigned to four stereo mix buses: **PGM**, **REC**, **AUX**, and **VBUS**.

## Inputs

- **Microphone (4 mono balanced)**
  With 48V phantom power, 4-band parametric equalizer, dynamic compressor, noise gate, and de-esser.
- **Line (4 stereo balanced)**
  With 3-band parametric equalizer.
- **UNIDEX (16 mono / 8 stereo)**
  Balanced inputs configurable as mono or stereo.
  Allow connection of UNIDEX adapters (microphones, telephone lines, Bluetooth interfaces, etc.).
  Include 3-band parametric equalizer, dynamic compressor, and noise gates.
- **USB (4 stereo digital inputs)**
  Compatible with ASIO 3.0. The connected computer recognizes four playback devices.
  Include 3-band parametric equalizer.
- **AES-3 (4 stereo digital inputs)**
  With 3-band parametric equalizer.
- **Incoming streaming**
  Not an independent input; when activated, it replaces AES-3 input #1.
- **External tuner**
  Dedicated input for transmission monitoring.

## Outputs

There are 18 stereo outputs and 4 monitoring outputs:

- **Program (PGM)**
  Dual balanced stereo analog output.
- **4 balanced stereo (assignable)**
- **4 unbalanced stereo (assignable)**
  Can be configured as up to 8 mono outputs. Include **mix-minus** option for telephone and VoIP communications.
- **USB (4 assignable stereo sends)**
  The computer connected via USB detects these outputs as recording devices.
- **AES-3 (4 digital outputs)**
  Two of them can be assigned to any of the buses.
- **Dual PCM/MP3 streaming**
  Simultaneous transmission via RTP and Icecast/Shoutcast.

## Monitoring and Intercom

- Monitoring outputs for Studio (speakers and headphones).
- Simplified monitoring connection for Studio with **Solidyne StudioBox** bays.
- Monitoring outputs for Control (speakers and headphones).
- Talkback to Studio.
- Selectable command microphone: internal (PZM) or external.
- 4 assignable intercom lines to audio outputs.
- **Reverse talkback**: allows speaking from Studio to the operator's CUE (requires Studiobox HD3 or HD5).
- CUE bus (Pre Fader Level) assignable to any output.

## External Device Triggering

Features:

- **4 GPI**
- **8 GPO** of open-collector type

Allows triggering:

- Solidyne Audicom automation
- Audio processors
- Other remotely controlled devices

Activation is programmable (see corresponding section).
