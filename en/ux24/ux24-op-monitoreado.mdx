## Monitoring Section

This section of the console assigns the signals that will be heard through the studio monitor speakers and control room headphones.\
Also located here are the master microphone buttons **A** and **B**, the **CUE** level control, the talk-back microphone, and the intercom groups.

<img
  src="/images/ux24/ux24-superficie-monitoreo.jpg"
  alt="UX24 Monitoring Area"
  title="UX24 Monitoring Area"
  style={{ width:"51%" }}
  className="mx-auto"
/>

The **selection controls** 1 and 2 are encoders with rotation and push functions.\
To select a signal source:

1. Rotate the encoder: the options will cycle on the display, flashing until confirmed.
2. Press the encoder to confirm: the audio switches at that moment.

If the knob is rotated but not pressed to confirm, after 10 seconds the change is canceled and the display returns to the previous source.

<Tip>
  If the encoder is pressed without rotating, it returns to the previous source (indicated below the current one in small text), allowing **quick switching** between two sources.
</Tip>

#### 1 <Icon icon="arrow-right-long" iconType="solid" color="#ffa300" size={18} /> Studio Monitor

Selects the source sent to the **STUDIO MONITOR** output.\
Options: `AIR-IN`, `PGM`, `REC`, `AUX`, `VBUS`, `CUE`.

- The level for studio speakers and headphones is adjusted from the touchscreen under the **MONITOR** option.

  This level is typically left fixed, with each participant adjusting their own level from a headphone distribution unit.
- In single-booth radio stations, monitor speakers may be connected to the **Control Room** output to utilize the physical volume knob.

#### 2 <Icon icon="arrow-right-long" iconType="solid" color="#ffa300" size={18} /> Control Room (Control Room Monitoring)

Allows selecting the source sent to the **CONTROL ROOM** outputs.\
Options: `AIR-IN`, `PGM`, `REC`, `AUX`, `VBUS`.

<Note>
  **Control Room Speaker Mute:**\
  When using a microphone in the Control Room, it can be configured to mute the control room speaker output upon activation (see _Channel Display_).\
  This is also used in single-studio or "hot studio" setups, connecting monitoring speakers to the **Control Room** output and configuring all microphones as "located in Control Room" to enable automatic muting.
</Note>

#### 3 <Icon icon="arrow-right-long" iconType="solid" color="#ffa300" size={18} /> Control Room Monitor Level

Rotary fader that adjusts the level of the speakers connected to the **Control Room** output.\
The output is line level for active or amplified speakers. The maximum level achieved will also depend on the adjustment of the monitors themselves.

#### 4 <Icon icon="arrow-right-long" iconType="solid" color="#ffa300" size={18} /> Control Room Headphones Level

Rotary fader for the operator's headphones level (connected to **Control Room Headphones**).

#### 5 <Icon icon="arrow-right-long" iconType="solid" color="#ffa300" size={18} /> CUE Level

Rotary fader for the **CUE** bus level.\
The main display shows a calibrated dBfs meter that reflects the level after the CUE control. If the fader is closed, there will be no indication.

The **SPEAKER** and **PHONES** buttons allow injecting the CUE signal into the Control Room speakers and/or headphones.

- When active, the CUE signal is summed with the monitored signal (shown on the SOURCE display).
- The main signal is automatically attenuated when a CUE signal is present.
- The attenuation level is configured in the **MONITOR → ADVANCED** screen.

The CUE bus can be assigned to any output for connecting auxiliary speakers. By default, it is assigned to **UNBAL 8**.

## Intercom and Talk-Back

### **INTERCOM**

The Intercom buttons (see item 6 in the image) are linked to audio outputs from the console to configure advanced talkback circuits. When an Intercom button is pressed, the talkback microphone audio interrupts the audio on the output associated with that Intercom.

<Tip>
  **Use cases:**

  - Set up differentiated headphone groups to speak to everyone (with Talk-Back) or to a specific group (with Intercom).
  - Set up a Talk-Back button for a Studio B.
</Tip>

<Note>
  **Using HD3 as Intercom**

  Download a guide to install a [Solidyne StudioBox HD3 as Intercom](http://solidyne.dx.com.ar/DW/doc-guides/Studiobox-HD3_as_UX24s_Intercom_set.pdf).
</Note>

### **TALK-BACK**

When this button is pressed, the Talk-Back microphone is sent to the STUDIO output (speakers and headphones). In headphones, Talk-Back is played on the left channel while maintaining the program signal (or other assigned signal) on the right channel.

## MASTER-MIC Buttons

Simultaneously activate all microphone channels associated with the button (A or B). \
Also triggers the corresponding Tally A or B signaling, and mutes the speakers on the STUDIO output (MASTER MIC A) or the audio output assigned to Studio B (MASTER MIC B).

### Studio Speaker

Adjusts the signal level for speakers connected to the **Studio Monitor** output.\
The output is line level, designed for active speakers.

### Studio Headphones

Adjust the signal level for headphones connected to the **Studio Monitor** output.\
It is designed to feed a headphone distribution amplifier.

<Warning>
  **Monitoring Level Note:**\
  The level of the studio's speaker and headphone outputs should be adjusted to prevent excessive amplification in active monitors or the headphone distribution amplifier.\
  If the console's signal is too low and subsequently amplified, the signal-to-noise ratio will degrade.
</Warning>

### On-Air Input Gain

Controls the gain of the **Air Monitor Input** (external tuner).

### Ring Level

Level of the "ring" tone in **CUE**, generated by the console when a call is received via landline or Bluetooth.

### Talkback Mic Level

Adjusts the talkback microphone level to Studio monitoring, Intercom, and telephone lines.

### Talkback Mic Source

Defines the microphone input used for talkback (Studios, TELCO, and Intercom).\
Options:

- Built-in ambient microphone.
- Microphone inputs 1 to 4.
- UNIDEX-4 L and UNIDEX-4 R.
- DANTE #1 (when available)

The input assigned to talkback can also be assigned to a channel, for example, to put that microphone on-air.

### Control Room CUE Sum Audio Att

Sets the attenuation of the signal being monitored in the Control Room when a CUE signal is mixed into speakers and/or headphones.\
This attenuation is also applied when activating a talk-back button (to studios or TELCO line) or an Intercom.

#### Intercom Input

Adjusts the signal level in **CUE** coming from **StudioBox** accessories (_reverse talkback_).

**Intercom to CUE:** enables or disables the Intercom return input to the CUE bus.
