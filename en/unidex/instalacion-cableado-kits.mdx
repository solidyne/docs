## Pre-assembled Cable Kits

Solidyne offers cable and adapter kits designed to connect the console without the need to assemble cables. The options:

- Generic pre-assembled cable kit for new installations.
- Generic adapter cable kit for connecting the UNIDEX in pre-existing installations.
- User-customized kit.
