The **UNIDEX** line from Solidyne covers the needs for operation and on-air operation in **FM, AM and streaming stations**. UNIDEX offers a digital workflow without the complexity of a dedicated IP network, but with the option for audio over IP (DANTE/AES67).

![Linea Unidex Pn](/images/thumbnails/Linea_unidex.png)

## UX18 and UX24 Models

The UNIDEX Series includes two mixing console models: **UX24** and **UX18**.

Both models share features and operating modes. To simplify the documentation, common aspects are explained in this section.

The [UX24](/en/ux24/ux24-summary) and [UX18](/en/ux18/ux18-summary) menu entries detail the specific operational and configuration particulars of each model.

---

## UDX Adapters

- **UNIDEX** stands for "UNIversal Desktop with EXternal adapters". They are external interfaces that connect directly to the console to input different types of signals (telecommunications, microphones, USB, etc.).
- The UNIDEX-type inputs on the console are balanced line inputs with expanded capabilities, which support the connection of UDX adapters.
- They connect directly to the console: no routers, switches, or networks are required.
- The adapters communicate data and function control from the console surface.

---

## Audio over IP (optional)

1. Enables advanced interconnection between devices and studios via an **audio network**.
2. Requires a **dedicated network** and compatible DANTE/AES67 input/output nodes.
3. Control via WEB interface from remote studios.

---

## Hardware

- Internal processing: Full-digital **24-bit @ 48 kHz** DSP.
- 100 mm soft-touch high-durability faders:\
  UX24: 12 physical faders \+ 12 virtual faders on 7” touchscreen\
  UX18: 10 physical faders \+ 8 virtual faders on 7” touchscreen
- UX24 expandable to 18 or 24 physical faders with UX6 panels (6 faders each).

---

## Connectivity

- All I/O is via **RJ45** on the rear panel.
- RJ45→XLR/TRS/RCA adapter cables are available.

---

## On-air operation

- **Plug & play**: factory-configured, ready to use.
- No advanced knowledge required.
- Operation similar to a traditional console.
- Audio processing is adjusted on the touchscreen.

---

## User Levels

1. **Basic**: access to minimal functions.
2. **Intermediate**: includes advanced options.
3. **Administrator**: full access to configuration and settings.

---

## Advanced Flexibility

- Assign **any input** to any of the physical faders or to the virtual ones.
- Create **scenes** with channel settings and processing.
- **Remote** control and configuration via IP (WEB browser). Allows setting up an auxiliary studio controlled from a screen, without needing an additional console.
- Use **VST effects** in real time via USB ASIO and an external computer.
- Supports up to two simultaneous on-air studios (UX24 model).

<Info>
  All functions and settings are managed from the touch screen or via IP from a browser on a tablet or computer.
</Info>
