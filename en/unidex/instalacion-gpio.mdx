## GPIO Control

The **GPIO** _(General Purpose Input/Output)_ connector provides inputs and outputs that allow control of console functions (GPI), such as microphone activation; or control of external devices (GPO), such as indicator lights or audio processing.

- **GPI** supports trigger signals of **5-15V/150mA** (active high).
- **GPO 1 to 7** are of the \_"open drain" type. \_They function as switches, connecting the output to ground. They support signals of **5–24 V / 250 mA**.
- **PIN 8** supplies **DC voltage** (differs depending on the console model, see below). It can be used to trigger GPIs directly (with a switch). Or to power an external device activated via a GPO (for example, a light or a small relay)

The actions triggered by the **GPIO** are defined on the `SETTINGS→GPIO` screen. Each **GP-IN** and **GP-OUT** can be associated with a specific channel and action.

The available actions for **GPI** are:

`UNASSIGNED; CHANNEL ON/OFF; CHANNEL MUTE; MASTER MIC A; MASTER MIC B`

The available actions for **GPO** are:

`UNASSIGNED; CHANNEL ON/OFF; TALLY A; TALLY B; TELCO RING; OUTPUT +12V` (only GPO8)

### GPIO ACTION CONFIGURATION

The actions that trigger the GPIO are defined in the `SETTINGS→GPIO` screen. Each **GP-IN** and **GP-OUT** can be associated with a channel and a specific action.

The available actions for **GPI** are:

`UNASSIGNED; CHANNEL ON/OFF;  CHANNEL MUTE;  MASTER MIC A`

The available actions for **GPO** are:

`UNASSIGNED;  CHANNEL ON/OFF;  TALLY A;  TALLY B;  TELCO RING`

</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Tip>
  **EXAMPLES**

  \
  The following configuration for GPI:  **GPI 1  |  CHANNEL ON/OFF  |  CHANNEL 1**

  ...will cause Channel 1 to turn on or off based on the control signal present at the **GPI 1** input.

  The following configuration for GPO: GPO 1  |   CHANNEL ON/OFF  |  CHANNEL 6

  ...will change the state of the **GPO 1** output based on whether Channel 6 is turned on or off.
</Tip>

## GPIO Application Cases

### LED Gooseneck Arm (GPO)

The **GPO 1 to 7** outputs can function as **electronic switches**, controlling LEDs connected to an external power supply, provided the current draw does not exceed **250 mA**.

<Danger>
  If the current draw exceeds **250 mA**, an **external relay** must be used for switching. It is recommended to use **GPO 8** to directly drive a 12 V coil relay with a current draw of up to 250 mA.
</Danger>

The figure shows an example of directly connecting an **LED gooseneck microphone arm**, using **GPO-1**.

In the **GPIO configuration**, the GPO outputs must be assigned to the ON/OFF function of the corresponding MIC channel.

Typical example:

- **GPO 1 →** ON/OFF MIC Channel 1
- **GPO 2 →** ON/OFF MIC Channel 2
- etc.

### Mute Microphone (GPI Usage)

The **GPI** inputs allow automated actions to be executed on the console.

One of the most useful is the temporary channel mute (MUTE), ideal for implementing a **cough button** in the studio. This allows the presenter to mute their microphone without operator intervention.

The following example shows the connection of a **momentary push button** to activate **GPI 1**.

<img
  src="/images/ux24/gpio-mute.png"

In this case:

- **GPI 1** is configured with the **MUTE** action and is associated with the desired channel.
- **GPO 8** delivers a fixed voltage.

<Warning>
  In **UX24**, configure GPO-8 for fixed voltage. In **UX18**, pin-8 always delivers fixed voltage.
</Warning>

During a GPI-activated mute, if the operator **moves the fader manually**, the console returns control to them, and the microphone automatically goes back on-air.

## "ON-AIR" Indicator Lights (Tally Light)

On the rear panel of the console is the **12 V / 250 mA output** for connecting **"ON-AIR"** indicator lights.

<Note>
  The UX24 model handles two studios and has two Tally Light outputs: Studio A and Studio B.
</Note>

- Up to **two Solidyne LA100 on-air lights** can be connected per output.
- For lights from other brands, ensure the total consumption **does not exceed 250 mA**.

<Warning>
  The on-air light outputs are protected by a **PTC (fusistor)** component that acts on temperature. If the current exceeds **250 mA**, the output is automatically disconnected to protect the console.

  To restore the output:

  1. Disconnect the load (the light).
  2. Check the system to identify the cause of the overconsumption (e.g., too many LEDs or a short circuit).
  3. Wait for the PTC to cool down before reconnecting.
</Warning>
