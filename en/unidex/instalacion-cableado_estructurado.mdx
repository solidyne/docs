## General Considerations

UNIDEX consoles use **RJ45** connectors for many of their inputs and outputs, employing **shielded CAT-5** multipair cable.

With the adoption of audio over IP (AoIP), the use of RJ45 connectors became standard, allowing multiple types of analog and digital connectors to be replaced by a single format. This simplifies installations and reduces the physical size of the consoles.

Structured cabling offers several advantages:

- A single type of cable is used for the entire installation.
- No soldering is required.
- It can carry analog signals, digital audio, or data.

To connect conventional audio devices (microphones, speakers, players, interfaces), **adapter cables** are used, going from a female RJ45 to the corresponding audio connector (XLR, TRS, RCA, etc.).

<Warning>
  RJ45 connectors are reliable and durable **only if the installation is done correctly**.

  - It is essential to avoid hanging cables that exert tension on the connectors.
  - **UDX adapters must be properly secured** and not left loose inside the rack or on the table.
  - It is recommended to use **cable channels or conduits** to organize the cabling.
  - For long runs, use RJ45-to-RJ45 cable and a short adapter from female RJ45 to the required audio connector.
  - Use good quality STP or SFTP structured cable.
</Warning>

<Warning>
  **Avoid mixing audio cables** with AC power cables. To prevent interference and hum, they must run on separate paths.
</Warning>

<Warning>
  If there is persistent hum and the grounding connections are correct, it may be due to **RF standing waves** induced by the FM antenna. In these cases, it is recommended to place **ferrite beads** on all cables entering the console.
</Warning>
