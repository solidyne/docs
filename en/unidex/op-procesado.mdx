## PROCESS SCREEN

To access, press **PROCESS** on the main screen. This also activates **SELECT mode** to choose a channel.

<img
  src="/images/ux24/ux24-pantalla-processs.jpg"
  alt="UNIDEX Pantalla Processs"
  title="UNIDEX Pantalla Processs"
  style={{ width:"65%" }}
  className="mr-auto"
/>

The processors are **fixed to each input**, not to the channel. They cannot be reassigned to other channels, buses, or outputs. The available processes depend on the **input type** assigned to the channel.

| **INPUT** | **EQ \+ Filters** | **Comp. \+ Gate** | **De-esser** |
| :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| MIC | 4 bands | **yes** | **yes** |
| UNIDEX | 3 bands | **yes** | no |
| USB | 3 bands | no | no |
| AES3 | 3 bands | no | no |
| BALANCED | 3 bands | no | no |

**Functions of the PROCESS screen**

- View which processors are on the channel and whether they are active or in **BYPASS**.
- Activate/deactivate a processor by touching **BYPASS**.
- Access its settings by touching the icon or the context button.

**Channel selection**

- In SELECT mode, use **CUE/SELECT** on the console or the **encoder** to scroll through the 24 channels (hardware channels in cyan, virtual channels in violet).
- Any action in PROCESS cancels SELECT, applying changes to the active channel.
- From PROCESS, you can also activate SELECT and choose another channel.

**Parameter adjustment:**\
It is performed by combining the **encoder** and the **touch options**, as indicated for each processor.

<Note>
  The processor settings can be **saved in the scene** .
</Note>

## PARAMETRIC EQUALIZER

<img
  src="/images/ux24/ux24-EQ.png"
  alt="UNIDEX EQ"
  title="UNIDEX EQ"
  style={{ width:"71%" }}
/>

**1 - NAME:** Indicates the channel number and name. Virtual channels show the name area in violet.

**2 - BANDS:** Band selection. The parameters for one band are displayed on screen at a time.\
Each band has a color that identifies it in the graph and in the parameter area.\
All inputs have 3 parametric bands, except for inputs MIC-1 to MIC-4 which have four bands.

**3 - CONTROLS:** Parameters for each EQ band. The background color changes with the selected band.

- **GAIN:** Gain in dB, with a range of ± 9 dB.
- **FREQ:** Adjusts the center frequency. All bands cover from 20 Hz to 20 kHz.
- **Q:** Width of the EQ bell curve. Determines the precision of the applied correction.
- **TYPE:** Band operating mode. Toggled by touching the TYPE option on screen.
  - Band #1: peak, low-shelf, or HPF.
  - Band #2 (and #3 on microphone inputs): peak or HPF/LPF.
  - Band #3 (#4 on mic inputs): peak, hi-shelf, or LPF.
- **ON/OFF:** Turns the band on/off. Toggled by touching ON/OFF on screen.

**4 - BY-PASS:** Disables the entire equalization stage. Also appears on the PROCESS screen.\
Toggled by touching BY-PASS on screen.

**5 - SELECT:** Activates channel selection mode.

**6 - BACK:** Returns to the previous screen.

### High-Pass / Low-Pass Filters

- Band #1 can function as a 2nd order (12 dB/oct) high-pass filter (HPF).
- Band #3 (Band #4 on MIC-1 to MIC-4 inputs) can function as a 2nd order (12 dB/oct) low-pass filter (LPF).
- Band #2 (and Band #3 on MIC-1 to MIC-4 inputs) can work as either an HPF or LPF.\
  If two bands are configured as high-pass or low-pass at the same frequency, the total cutoff attenuation will be **24 dB/oct**.

## DYNAMICS

The **DYNAMIC** processor includes a **noise gate** and a dynamic **compressor**.

<img
  src="/images/ux24/ux24-dynamics.jpg"
  alt="UNIDEX Dynamics"
  title="UNIDEX Dynamics"
  style={{ width:"65%" }}
/>

**1 - COMPRESSION CURVE:** Graphically displays the input-output relationship of the dynamic processor, including the gate and the compressor.

**2 - INPUT LEVEL:** Indicates the peak level of the signal at the compressor input (dBfs).

### 3 - Noise Gate (GATE)

- **Threshold:** Gate activation threshold (dB).
- **Attenuation:** Attenuation applied to the signal when the gate is active.
- **Style:** Changes the recovery time between preset values.
- **Active:** Indicator that lights up red when the gate is closed.

### 4 - Dynamic Compressor

Controls for adjusting:

- Threshold
- Ratio
- Attack time
- Release time
- Hold
- Output gain

**5 - BYPASS:** Completely disables the dynamic processing stage (gate + compressor).

## DE-ESSER

<img
  src="/images/ux24/ux24-deesser.jpg"
  alt="UNIDEX De-esser"
  title="UNIDEX De-esser"
  style={{ width:"65%" }}
/>

The microphone inputs feature a **de-esser** for sibilance reduction.\
This processor works as a multiband compressor, attenuating only the defined frequency range when the signal level exceeds the set threshold.

**Controls:**

- **1 - Type:** Preset adjustments.
- **2 - Frequency start:** Lower cutoff frequency. Adjusted according to signal characteristics. Usually between 4,500 and 5,500 Hz.
- **3 - Frequency end:** Upper cutoff frequency. Adjusted according to signal characteristics. Usually between 7,000 and 12,000 Hz.
- **4 - Threshold:** Threshold level in dB. When the signal in the working band exceeds this value, it is attenuated with a **fixed ratio of 15:1**.
- **5 - Gain reduction:** Indicates the applied level reduction, in dB.
