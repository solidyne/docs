## Microphone Inputs

The MX2100 has 3 microphone inputs (MIC-1, MIC-2, and MIC-3) with **1/4" (6.25 mm) TRS** connectors.

The preamplifiers use the *local feedback* technique, which prevents saturation in the presence of high sound pressure levels (for example, a commentator shouting a goal).

### Built-in Microphone

- Electret microphone built into the front of the unit (MIC-4).
- Allows going on-air even without external microphones.
- Shares the level control with the line input and Bluetooth return.

<Warning>
  MIC-4 is disabled if the USB connection (to a computer or charger) is used or if the line input is connected.\
  The **MIC-4/LIN/Bluetooth** control presents different signals depending on the operating scenario (see *Console Operation*).
</Warning>

---

## Line Input

- **LIN-4:** mono line input.
- Accepts direct connection from headphone outputs of portable recorders, mobile phones, notebooks, tablets, etc.
- A 1/8" stereo miniplug cable can be used, although in the console the ring (right channel) remains unconnected.
- Shares level control with the built-in microphone (MIC-4) and the Bluetooth return.

<Warning>
  If stereo equipment is connected, the input will use only one channel. The plug's ring (right channel) remains unconnected in the console.
</Warning>

## Program Output (Bal Out)

- Balanced, mono output with **TRS 1/8"** connector.
- Mix of signals: MIC-1, MIC-2, MIC-3/USB and MIC-4/LINE/Bluetooth.

### Dynamic Processing

The PGM signal passes through a dynamic range compressor designed for human voice:

- Controls peaks and prevents level differences.
- Distortion‑free with no increase in background noise.
- Compression indicated by two front‑panel LEDs.

This system allows interviews to be conducted with consistent levels, without requiring an audio operator.

The PGM output can be used to:

- Connect to portable digital recorders and obtain high‑quality recordings.
- Send signal to studios via VHF/UHF radio link in high quality (30–15,000 Hz).

## Headphone Outputs

- Internal headphone signal distribution.
- 3 outputs with 3.5 mm minijack connectors.
- The mix sent to the headphones depends on the connection mode and work scenario (see _Console Operation_).

## Audio USB

The **MX2100** console was designed to simplify connection with laptops, enabling Internet streaming transmissions.

- The **USB** connection handles both **sends** and **returns** of the signal with Studios.
- A standard **mini-USB to USB-A male** cable is used.

### Automatic Operating System Detection

When connecting the MX2100 to a computer running **Windows 7/8/10**, **Linux**, or **OS-X**:

- The operating system detects the console and automatically installs the necessary drivers.
- No additional drivers are required.

<Info>
  On Windows, the USB device is automatically configured as the **default device**.
</Info>

### Detected Devices

The console is identified as:

- **USB playback device (stereo out):** the computer sends audio to the MX2100.
- **USB recording device (stereo in):** the computer receives audio from the MX2100.

## USB Power Supply

- Internal rechargeable Ni-Cd battery.
- Battery life: 16 hours of continuous use thanks to CMOS technology.
- Automatic multi-voltage external USB charger (100–240 VAC) included.
- Connection via USB-A to mini-USB cable.

### Charging Time and Method

- Time for full charge: ~12 hours.
- Above that value, the charging current decreases and it can be left connected without risk.
- Red LED (CHG) indicates charging in progress.
- The LED remains lit while the unit is connected to the charger, even when powered off.
- It can also be recharged by connecting it via USB to a computer.
