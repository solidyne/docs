The console connects to the phone line via the **RJ11 “Cellular & Phone”** connector, using a standard telephone cable.

- It incorporates a **DTMF** keypad, so an additional telephone is not required.
- The **program mix** is sent to the radio station via the line.
- The **return from Studios** is received on the same line and is added directly to the headphone mix.
