## Connecting a Mobile Phone via Bluetooth

The MX2100 supports connecting a mobile phone via **Bluetooth**, for transmission to studios or to add a mobile phone to the broadcast when the link is made from an external computer or land line (POTS).

- Compatible with any phone with Bluetooth.
- Eliminates dependence on hands-free cables, which vary by brand and model.
- Maximum distance: up to 5 meters; recommended: keep the mobile phone on your person but separate from the console (shirt pocket or belt on the opposite side).
- Advantage: the signal remains digital from the mobile phone to inside the MX2100, achieving better audio quality.

---

### Pairing a Mobile Phone to the Console

The initial pairing only needs to be done once per phone. After that, the connection is automatic when Bluetooth is activated on both devices.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn off Bluetooth on the console">
    Verify that the Bluetooth LED (located on the DTMF keypad) is off.

    - If it flashes green every 2 seconds → it is on.
    - To turn it off: hold down the **"#" and "9"** keys until the LED turns off.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate search mode on the console">
    With Bluetooth off, hold down **"#" and "9"** for ~10 seconds until the LED flashes rapidly alternating green/red.\
    This indicates the MX2100 has entered search mode.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search for devices on the mobile phone">
    On the phone, start a search for Bluetooth devices.\
    The procedure depends on the model; consult the mobile phone's manual.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the console">
    The phone will display the device **Solidyne BTM641** (on older models: BTM751).

    - If it requests a PIN: enter **0000**.
    - The MX2100's ID is stored in the mobile phone.
    - The console's LED changes to a slow green flash (one flash every 2 seconds), indicating an active connection.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Bluetooth is turned on by holding "#" and "9" (or any combination between rows 3 and 4) for ~5 seconds, releasing when the green LED lights up.

  - Slow green = on.
  - Fast green/red = search mode.
</Warning>

<Note>
  On some phones, after detecting the device, it is necessary to press **"connect"** for it to become active. Others activate it automatically.
</Note>

---

### Reconnect Paired Device

To reconnect a previously paired mobile phone:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn on Bluetooth on the console">
    Press **“#” and “9”** until the LED lights green (approx. 5 seconds).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on Bluetooth on the phone">
    When the mobile phone confirms connection, it is ready to operate.\
    When making or receiving calls, the audio is sent to the console.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  On some phones, reconnection is not automatic and the **Solidyne BTM641** device must be manually selected from the list of Bluetooth devices.
</Note>

---

### Volume Adjustment

- Adjust the Bluetooth volume on the mobile phone to **maximum**, to achieve the best signal-to-noise ratio.
- The adjustment must be made **during a call** via Bluetooth.
- The phone's volume is independent of the Bluetooth volume.
- The value is stored in the mobile phone's memory.
- If you change phones, you must readjust the level to maximum.

<Warning>
  If you adjust the volume without being on a Bluetooth call, you are only changing the phone's speaker volume, **not** the Bluetooth volume.
</Warning>

---

### Turn Off Bluetooth on the Console

To avoid battery consumption when not in use, turn off Bluetooth by holding down **"#" and "9"** until the LED turns off completely.

<Accordion title="Mobile Phone Connection via Cable (Obsolete)" icon="mobile">
  The **RJ-11 "Cellular&Phone"** connector allows connecting a mobile phone using a **hands-free** accessory.

  - It is used as a backup for the external computer link, especially when Bluetooth is in use to send another mobile phone on-air.
  - The mobile phone transmits the microphone (tip of the miniplug) and speaker (ring) signals to the MX2100.
  - The console sends the program signal to the mobile phone, which retransmits it to the radio.
  - The return from the radio arrives at the mobile phone and from there to the console, integrating into the headphone mix (STUDIO).
  - While using this connector, the mobile phone's microphone and speaker are disconnected.
