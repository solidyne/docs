## Front Panel

### Power On

The unit is powered on by pressing the **ON** button, whether it is connected to the charger or operating on internal battery.

<Note>
  When the unit is connected via USB, to the charger, or to a computer, the charge indicator LED (**CHG**) remains lit even when the console is off.\
  Battery life: 16 hours of continuous use.\
  Total charging time: 12 hours.\
  The charging system automatically reduces the current when it reaches 100%, so it can remain connected indefinitely.
</Note>

<Warning>
  Remember to turn off the console after use to avoid unnecessary battery consumption.
</Warning>

## Program Mixer

The program signal (**PGM**) is generated from the sum of the sources: **MIC-1, MIC-2, MIC-3, MIC-4 (built-in), LINE, Bluetooth, and USB**.\
Some inputs share controls and their availability depends on the console's connection method.

- The PGM signal is sent to the radio via telephone or computer with Internet.
- It is also present at the **BAL OUT** connector on the rear panel.

**MIC-3/USB Control**

Switches between the MIC-3 input or the computer connected to the USB. When the MX2100 is used connected to a computer for Internet transmission, a microphone **must be connected to MIC-3**. In this condition, USB does not enter the PGM mix, but it is heard in the headphones as a direct return.

**MIC-4/LIN/Bluetooth Control**

Handles the signal from the built-in mic, the LINE input, or the Bluetooth device; depending on the operating condition:

- MIC-4 signal is available if USB or the line input is **NOT** being used.
- LINE signal is available only when LIN-4 is connected (overrides MIC-4).
- Bluetooth return is available in the PGM mix only when the console is connected via USB (scenario of transmission from the external computer).

## Headphones

The headphone mix varies depending on the usage mode:

- **(a) Telephone communication (land line or Bluetooth):**
  - PGM mix: MIC-1, MIC-2, MIC-3, and Line/MIC-4.
  - Direct return from Studios (hybrid or Bluetooth).
  - Ring tone on incoming calls.
- **(b) USB and external computer with unidirectional streaming:**
  - PGM mix: MIC-1, MIC-2, USB, and Bluetooth (used to bring another mobile phone on-air).
  - Return from USB (return from Studios is not used due to streaming delay).
- **(c) USB and external computer with real-time link (VoIP):**
  - PGM mix: MIC-1, MIC-2, MIC-3, and LINE.
  - USB return (Studios).
  - Bluetooth return.
  - Hybrid return.

<Note>
  The field reporter hears themselves through the program mix.\
  The operator in Studios does not need to forward their signal.
</Note>

<Warning>
  The noise floor in the headphones increases if the hybrid is active (the **ON-AIR** button is pressed).\
  Verify that it is not pressed when the hybrid is not in use.
</Warning>

## Level Indicator

- Dual LED indicator shows the action of the internal compressor.
- Green LED: low level, no compression.
- Red LED: indicates compression.
- The greater the compression, the higher the intensity of the red LED.

<Note>
  The proper operating level is achieved when signal peaks occasionally activate the red LED.
</Note>

---

### Compressor Action

The MX2100 incorporates an **automatic compressor + limiter**, which keeps signal peaks constant at the output.

- Improves the signal-to-noise ratio.
- Ensures uniformity across different transmission channels.
