In this section you can request the **Service Manual**, which includes:

- Circuit diagrams
- Component layout
- Technical information for diagnosis and repair

Because some units have different hardware versions, it is necessary to correctly identify the unit before sending the corresponding documentation.

## Procedure for Requesting Technical Documentation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Send the Information via Email">
    Send an email to `info@solidyne.ar` attaching:

    - A photograph of the equipment
    - Equipment model
    - Serial number
    - Name of the station or company
    - Address and country
    - Clear description of the detected problem

    The more precise the technical description, the more efficient the support process will be.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay the Fee">
    Along with the confirmation of receipt of the information sent, the **cost of the requested documentation** will be provided, along with a link to make the payment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receipt of the Manual">
    Within **2 business days** after payment confirmation, the requested **Service Manual in PDF format** will be sent by email.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Confidentiality of Information

<Note>
  Receipt of the Service Manual implies acceptance of maintaining the **confidentiality of the supplied technical information**.

  The documentation must be shared exclusively with the technician responsible for repairing the equipment and must not be publicly distributed.
</Note>
