## Key Features HL240-VQ

### Connectivity

- **Land lines (POTS)**: 2 analog hybrids with adjustable rejection.
- **Bluetooth 4.0**: two independent channels with HSP/HFP/A2DP profiles.
- **VoIP compatibility**: via ATA adapters or VoIP phones with Bluetooth.
- **Solidyne console interface**: direct hybrid send/return connection (2300, 2600, DX816/822, D612 series).

### Inputs and Outputs

- **AUDIO IN input (Send to Lines)**: unbalanced, for mix-minus feed from the console.
- **AUDIO OUT output (Mix)**: balanced XLR, delivers the total line mix.
- **Direct outputs PH-1 and BLT-1**: unbalanced, pre-fader, with fixed level.
- **Headphone output**: direct monitoring of the main mix.

### Audio Processing

- **VQR (Voice Quality Restoration)**: bass reconstruction up to 50 Hz and treble up to 12 kHz.
- **NOISE Control**: noise gate to reduce background noise during conversation pauses.

### Operation

- **Illuminated buttons**: line status and ring cadence.
- **Integrated talkback**: allows private conversation with the caller.
- **Priority system**: attenuates line audio by 10 dB when local voice is detected.
- **Conference**: internal mixing of up to 4 lines (2 POTS + 2 Bluetooth).

### Performance

- **Frequency response**: 50 Hz – 12 kHz with VQR // 300 Hz – 3.4 kHz without VQR.
- **Signal-to-noise ratio**: 70 dBA with VQR.
- **THD**: < 0.2 %.
- **Hybrid rejection**: > 40 dB (measured with 2 km artificial line).

### Construction and Power Supply

- **19" rack-mount format**: 1U height. Includes rubber feet for desktop use.
- **Internal stabilized power supply**: Switchable 110/220 V, 20 W power consumption.
- **Surge protection**: Metal oxide varistors rated for up to 2,000 V.
